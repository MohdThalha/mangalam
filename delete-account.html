Delete your Mangalam account

Mangalam · Multi-Vendor Platform · app ID in.mangalam.app

This page explains how to delete your Mangalam account and what happens to your data when you do. Mangalam is operated by Mohamed Thalgha, Tamil Nadu, India.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open the Mangalam app.
  2. Tap Account in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Read what will be removed, then tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out on every device. There is nothing else to do and nothing to wait for.

Or ask us to delete it

If you cannot open the app, write to mangalamapp26@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or send us the mobile number you signed in with. We acknowledge every request within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your name, mobile number and email address Deleted. The mobile number is released, so it can be used to register a new account.
Saved delivery addresses, including any location you shared Deleted.
Your sessions on every device Ended immediately.
Marketing preferences Deleted, and no further messages are sent.
Reviews you wrote Kept, but no longer linked to you.
Past orders: items, amounts and the address that order went to Kept as the shop's sales record, no longer linked to your account. Indian tax and accounting law requires a seller to keep these records, so they are not ours alone to erase.

One thing to finish first

An account cannot be deleted while an order is still in progress. Wait for it to be delivered, or cancel it, and then delete the account.
