Terms of Service

Mangalam · Last updated: 28 September 2026

These terms apply when you use the Mangalam app. By placing an order you accept them. The app is operated by Mohamed Thalgha, an individual based in Mettupalayam, Tamil Nadu, India ("we", "us").

1. What Mangalam is

Mangalam is a marketplace. It lists local shops and lets you order from them. We do not sell any goods ourselves. Each order is a contract between you and the shop you ordered from. The shop sets its prices, owns and describes its stock, packs the order and delivers it.

2. Your account

You must be 18 or over to hold an account. You need an Indian mobile number to use the app, and you confirm it with a code sent by SMS. Keep your phone and the codes to yourself; anyone who has them can order as you. Tell us at once if you think somebody else has access to your account. One person may hold one account, and the details you give must be accurate.

3. Orders, prices and payment

  • Prices, pack sizes, delivery charges and minimum order values are set by each shop and are shown before you confirm an order.
  • Payment is cash on delivery, paid directly to the shop when it hands over your order. We never collect the money and never hold it.
  • An order is confirmed only when the shop accepts it. A shop may decline an order, for example when an item has sold out.
  • Fresh produce, meat and fish vary in weight. Where an item is sold by weight, the final amount may differ slightly from the estimate shown.
  • You confirm delivery by giving the shop the four-digit code shown in the app. Do not share that code before you have received your order.

4. Cancellations, returns and complaints

You may cancel an order in the app while it is still allowed to be cancelled, which is generally before the shop has packed it. Because no money changes hands until delivery, a cancellation costs you nothing.

If something arrives damaged, spoiled, wrong or missing, refuse it at the door or tell the shop straight away, and the shop will put it right. As the seller, the shop is responsible for the goods, including their quality, weight, labelling and any refund. If a shop does not deal with a complaint fairly, contact us at the address below and we will take it up with them, and we may remove shops that repeatedly fail their customers.

5. What may be sold, and what may not

Shops list their own stock, and they are responsible for it. The following may never be listed or sold through Mangalam:

  • Prescription medicines — anything under Schedule H, H1 or X of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, or any medicine a pharmacist may only hand over against a doctor's prescription. See section 6.
  • Alcohol, tobacco, pan masala, gutkha and nicotine products in any form.
  • Narcotics, psychotropic substances, and anything whose sale is an offence.
  • Weapons, ammunition, explosives and fireworks.
  • Live animals and birds.
  • Counterfeit goods, and goods a shop has no right to sell.
  • Expired stock, or food past its use-by date.
  • Unpackaged goods sold without the weight, price and other particulars the Legal Metrology Act requires.

We remove listings that break this list as soon as we find them, and we may suspend or close the shop that posted them. A shop that does this more than once is removed from Mangalam.

6. Medicines and health products

Medicines are not sold through Mangalam. The Medical & Pharmacy category is switched off: no pharmacy is listed, and no medicine — prescription or over-the-counter — can be ordered in the app. Buy medicine at the counter, in person.

If pharmacies are added later, it will be for over-the-counter items only, every shop will have to hold a valid retail drug licence under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, and this page will say so before it happens.

Nothing in the app is medical advice. Product descriptions come from the shop or the manufacturer. If you are unwell, ask a doctor or a pharmacist. In an emergency, go to a hospital — do not place an order and wait.

7. Telling us about a problem listing

If you see something that should not be here — a prescription medicine, an item on the banned list above, a counterfeit product, expired stock, a misleading price or an offensive description — tell us, using the email or WhatsApp number at the end of this page, or through Account → Help centre in the app. Send the shop's name and what you saw, and a screenshot if you have one.

We look at every report. Anything unlawful or unsafe is taken down first and discussed afterwards, and we may suspend the shop while we look into it.

8. What the service costs

Using the app is free for customers. Delivery charges, where a shop applies them, are shown before you confirm an order and are paid to the shop. Shops pay us nothing during the introductory period. If we introduce charges for shops later, we will tell them in advance. This does not change the price you see before you confirm an order.

9. Using the app properly

Do not use the app to place false or malicious orders, to harass shopkeepers or delivery staff, to copy or scrape its content, to interfere with its security, or to break any law. Reviews must describe your own genuine experience. We may suspend or close an account that does these things.

If you run a shop here, you also confirm that you hold every licence and registration your trade requires — an FSSAI registration for food, a retail drug licence for a pharmacy, and any other licence that applies — that they stay valid, and that you will show them to us on request. You are the seller: your prices, weights, labels, stock and the goods themselves are your responsibility, as is following the Legal Metrology Act, food-safety law and the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. Orders must only be delivered to the address given, and the customer's details must only be used to fulfil that order.

11. Our responsibility

We are responsible for running the app itself. We are not the seller, and we are not responsible for the quality, safety, weight or fitness of the goods, or for a shop's delivery — those are the shop's responsibility as the seller under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and food-safety law. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under Indian consumer law that cannot be limited by agreement.

12. Closing your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app, under Account → Delete my account. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to your data.

15. Complaints and grievance officer

For a problem with an order, tell the shop first — it is the seller and can usually put it right fastest. If that does not work, or if the complaint is about Mangalam itself, write to the grievance officer below.

We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within one month of receiving it, as the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 require. Tell us your order number, what happened and what you would like done.
