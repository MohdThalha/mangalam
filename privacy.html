Privacy Policy

Mangalam · Last updated: 20 September 2026

Mangalam is a mobile application that connects shoppers with local grocery, meat, fish and fresh-food shops in Tamil Nadu. Shops receive the orders and deliver them themselves, and customers pay those shops in cash on delivery.

This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can have it removed. It applies to the Mangalam Android app and the service behind it.

The app is operated by Mohamed Thalgha, an individual based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India (referred to below as "we" or "us").

1. What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Mobile number It is your account. A code is sent to it to confirm the number belongs to you, and the shop needs it to contact you about your delivery.
Name (and email, if you add one) So the shop knows who the order is for.
Delivery addresses, including any location you choose to share from your device To show which shops deliver to you, to calculate the delivery fee, and to give the shop somewhere to deliver. Location is only read when you use the "use my current location" feature, and you can always type the address instead.
Orders: what you bought, from which shop, when, the amount, and the delivery address for that order To run the order, show your order history, and keep the shop's sales records.
Ratings and reviews you write Shown publicly in the app with your first name.
Device and diagnostic data: device model, Android version, app version, and crash reports To find and fix crashes. Crash reports do not include your address, phone number or order contents.

We do not collect payment card or bank details. Orders are paid in cash directly to the shop that delivers them, so no payment information passes through the app.

2. Who your data is shared with

The shop you order from receives your name, mobile number, delivery address and the items you ordered. It needs all of these to prepare and deliver the order. Shops may only use these details to fulfil your order.

We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase — sends the SMS code that confirms your mobile number, and delivers notifications.
  • Supabase and Render — database and server hosting.
  • Sentry — crash reporting.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.

3. Notifications and messages

We send an SMS containing a code when you sign in. We send notifications about your orders, such as when a shop accepts one or a delivery is on its way. Promotional messages are only sent if you have turned them on, and you can turn them off at any time in the app under Preferences.

4. How long we keep it

Your account details are kept while your account is open. Order records are kept for as long as the law requires them to be kept for tax and accounting purposes, even after an account is closed.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: open Account → Delete my account. You can also ask us to delete it by writing to the email address below.

When an account is deleted, your name, mobile number, email address and saved addresses are removed, and your mobile number is freed so it can be used to register again. Past orders remain in the shop's sales records as required for accounting, but are no longer linked to your account. Deletion is not possible while an order is still in progress.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it if it is wrong, delete it, or withdraw consent you have given. Write to the email address below and we will respond within a reasonable period.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords, where an account has one, are stored hashed and never in plain text. Access to the production database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data and will tell affected users about a breach that puts their data at risk.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
